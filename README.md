# Sales-performance-analysis

## Introduction:
This is a sales performance analysis across 6 different countries and 53 states within the period of 6 years. This analysis aims to show how much this company [un-named] have performed across her various branches in the different regions of the world. It also tends to find out how to optimize the sales, reduce cost and increase the revenue.

## Skills Utilized:
- Data collection
- Domain knowledge
- Functions and Formulas in Excel
- Data preparation and processing
- Data visualization and reporting

## Problem statement:
The issue of sales (revenue) trend and profit growth is the main underlining factor. Additionally, there was an underlying factor to determine how each product category, sub category and product performed across these key factors;
a.  Profit
b. Revenue
c. Customer base
d. Product

## Key findings

### Profit:
- The total profit made from the top 10 products is $11.4M. Mountain-200 black, 38 is the product with the highest profit while Mountain-200 black, 42 is the least.
- As much as Australia generated the highest average revenue at $893.01, there was no parallel increase in average profit, which was $283.98 (i.e. 21.03%). 
- Just as there was a 23.51% increase in revenue, there was also a 23.40% increase in the profit, suggesting a good fit for the sales years.

### Revenue:
-  Road Bikes made the top of the Product Sub Category that generated the best average revenue.
- Australia generated the best average revenue at $893.01 (32.5%) while Canada was the least country to generate average revenue.
- California made the top of the list for states that generated the top revenues at $17.5M, leading to a 25.6% impact  in the revenue generated.
- There was a 23.51% increase in the revenues generated along the years of sales analysed from 2011 to 2016.

## Customer base:
-  California had the highest customer at 22,220, followed by British Columbia at 13,965.
-  The least average age of the customers who are youths was 21y/o, the highest average age of customers who are seniors was 70y/o. The total average age of the customers was 36 years old which fell under the young adults age group.
- The Adults age group was the best performing age group having a 49.95% for revenue generate and 50.66% for profit. The Seniors were the worst performing age group having a 0.36% for revenue generated and 0.43% profit actualized.

## Product:
- The Bikes were the best performing product category for both Profit and Revenue. The Clothing category performed the least.
- Road bikes made the top of the Product Sub Category that generated the best average revenue.
- Mountain Bikes  ($922.16) generated the highest of the average profits made from the top 10 product sub category.

## Conclusion & Recommendation
- For Profit; Althought we have a 23.40% increase in profit growth, there is need to look into the products sold as only the top 10 made almost 10% of the profit actualized. Hence, I will suggest the services of a product analyst and competent marketer be utilized to better carry out market surveys to boost the presence of the the products.

- For Revenue; There was a 23.51% increase in revenue generated which suggest a good fit. However, aside Australia (for country) and California (for states), other regions performed relatively poor. More effort in meeting up with the marketing  will see to the increment of the clothing and accessories categories.

- For Customer-Base; The age group who patronized the company were mostly Adults. Therefore, there is need to improve the targetted campaigns strategies to be inclusive of other groups especially the seniors.

- For Products;  Since the bulk of the profit and revenue were made from the Bikes Category, I will suggest that marketing campaign strategies be focused mostly on the clothing and accessories categories, to boost sales from these categories.
